✦ glossary
Mamefriends
A series of avatars based on Mamehinata. They all share the same body, so any Mamefriend-compatible clothing will fit all of them.
Komado
A body type shared by Rusk, Karin, Mint, and Milk. Clothes made for any of them will fit all Komado avatars.
PlusHead
A body shared across several avatars. PlusHead clothes are cross-compatible between all avatars that use it.
Mochifitter
A tool that lets clothes from one avatar fit onto another, as long as a profile exists for it. For example, a Manuka outfit could potentially be made to fit Mamehinata.
